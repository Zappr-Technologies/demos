A Jayanagar kiosk that guests visit for filter coffee, then stay for the house seating.

The Google listing places Leesmith's at 729, 38th Cross, 16th A Main, 4th T Block East, Pattabhirama Nagar, Jayanagar, opposite Cosmopolitan Dental Clinic (a Native crop shop is also mentioned as a neighbour). Hours are 6 AM–11:30 PM every day. An August 2025 review called it one of the best recent filter coffees, noted a small kiosk, fast service, and coffee “in big coffee glass.” A June 2025 review described a house converted to a café sitting area, suitable for groups of 4–10, with Kannada, Hindi and English books, and said it stayed open till 11:45 pm — while also noting that “Price seems high,” “Staff is just ok,” and “Cleanliness -7/10.” A December 2025 review liked the coffee and lemon tea, called the place “tight cozy,” and suggested a more traditional seat arrangement. We have not published a menu or prices.

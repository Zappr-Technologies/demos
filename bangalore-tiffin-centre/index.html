A short, honest breakfast counter on 54th Cross.

The Google listing places Bangalore Tiffin Centre (Idly and Uddina Vada only) at 207, 54th Cross Road, 4th Block, Rajajinagar, with hours of 7–11 am every day. An August 2025 review said “these 2 are the only available food there” and praised “unlimited chutney.” A May 2025 review wrote that “Chutney is top notch” and “idli just melts in mouth,” while noting the counter is “a bit pricey” and that you will need another hotel if you want a wider breakfast. A September 2025 one-star review liked the idlis but called the chutney watery, said a staff member refused a spoon, and described a “dirty and unhygienic” sink. We have left that on the record. Prices are not listed on this demo.

A café that reviewers call warm on arrival.

The Google listing places Mugshot Cafe Tea Bench in 4th T Block East, Jayanagar, and classifies it as a café. In a September 2025 review, a visitor wrote that they were greeted with warmth and professionalism and that the ambience is cozy yet vibrant — perfect for catching up with friends or enjoying a quiet solo meal. Another reviewer in October 2025 praised hygienic organic dishes served with great love and respect, and named a staff member, Sanjay, for hospitality.

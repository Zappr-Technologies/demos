A Udayaravi Road boutique that guests keep returning to for blouses.

The Google listing places Her Majesty Woven at MIG-4, Udayaravi Road, 1st Stage, Kuvempunagar, open 11 AM–8 PM every day. A public Instagram account, @her_majesty_woven, is linked from the listing. October 2025 reviews praise timely (sometimes early) dress stitching, and blouse work attributed to Amulya and the team — including one guest who “given almost 10 blouses” after a successful trial, and another who received “intricate work” in three days. We have not treated three days as a standard promise. Prices were not published on the listing we checked.

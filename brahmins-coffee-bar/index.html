A short menu, kept on purpose.

The public Google listing places Brahmins' Coffee Bar at Pushp Kiran, 19 Ranga Rao Road, near Shankar Mutt Road in Shankarapura, and describes a simple room serving idli, vada and coffee. In July 2022, The Hindu wrote that the eatery was set up on 27 January 1965 by K. V. Nagesh Rao and K. N. Saraswati, and that the menu has remained coffee, khara bath, kesari bath, idli and vada, served with a signature coconut chutney. The listing shows morning hours and a Sunday close; please reconfirm timings before you visit.

What the listing and regulars talk about

These are dishes and details named on the Google listing, in recent reviews, or in The Hindu's 2022 profile — not a priced menu.

Idli & vada

Recent Google reviews mention soft idlis, crisp vada that sells quickly, and a tangy coconut chutney. One regular still puts this plate at the top of their Bengaluru list.

Khara & kesari bath

The Hindu recorded both as part of the original, unchanged menu. A November 2025 Google review described the kesari bath as gently sweet, with pineapple pieces.

Filter coffee

Listed as a coffee shop as well as a breakfast room. Reviews of the brew are mixed, which we have not polished over — the coffee is part of the ritual.

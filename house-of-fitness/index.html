A fourth-floor gym in Vijayanagar that members compare favourably with busier chains.

The Google listing places The House Of Fitness on the 4th floor, 4/58, 5th Main Road, above LIC, North Vijayanagar. Hours are 5 AM–10 PM Monday to Saturday and 7 AM–1 PM on Sunday. A November 2025 review called the space clean and uncrowded, praised a focus on “stability, mobility, and proper form,” and named trainers Karthik and Ashik, “certified and highly skilled, even training for HYROX-style workouts.” Another guest mentioned monthly assessments to track progress.
